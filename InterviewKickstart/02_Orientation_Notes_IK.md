# Interview Kickstart (IK) Orientation Notes
## Document: 02_Orientation_IK

---

## 1. Breakdown of IK Program Paths

IK classifies career advancement into specialized tracks depending on individual target outcomes:

* **Upskilling:** For professionals aiming to deepen technical skills in their current trajectory.
* **Promotion:** Not a standalone track, but achieved as a collateral benefit of completing other advanced courses.
* **Upleveling:** Handled via the **Interview Prep Lab** (includes a self-paced component).
* **Reskilling & Switchup Programs:** Tailored for individuals looking to entirely pivot or switch functional engineering roles.

## 2. Program Myths vs. Realities

| Myth | Reality |
| :--- | :--- |
| Simply attending live classes guarantees success. | Class attendance must be combined with continuous **Practice + Reflection**. The curriculum is demanding and intensive. |
| IK is a new or small platform. | Founded in **2014** with an established network of **25k+ Alumni**. |
| The program is strictly 1-on-1. | Organized around a **full cohort with live classes and interim feedback**. Personalized 1-on-1 access is reserved for coaching, mock interviews, and readiness validation. *Note: Due to the remote format, the daily workflow will feel self-driven.* |

| I will be interview-ready within 2–3 months. | Building true confidence requires non-stop effort over **many months**. The program is a long-term marathon. |

## 3. Applied GenAI & Agentic Systems Track

### 🎯 Strategic Focus & Core Philosophy
The curriculum focuses entirely on **Applied AI**—teaching you how to design and orchestrate intelligent software systems leveraging existing foundation models, rather than constructing the basic models themselves.

### ✅ What the Program Covers
* **Real-world Iteration:** Building and debugging live systems with **6+ guided projects** that mirror production workflows.
* **Agentic AI Architecture:** Comprehensive training on multi-agent planning, system orchestration, rigorous evaluation frameworks, and production-grade design patterns.
* **Interview Articulation:** Practice explaining complex agentic trade-offs, architectural strategies, and design decisions to clear top-tier AI interviews.
* **Hybrid Preparation:** Blends modern AI system engineering with traditional, role-specific interview preparation.

### ❌ What the Program Excludes
* Training large language models (LLMs) from raw, unformatted data.
* Designing or researching novel neural network architectures.
* Theoretical, math-heavy machine learning research.
* Large-scale distributed model training or low-level parameter optimization.
* Competing on baseline model quality with foundation model providers.

## 4. The Two Program Phases

1.  **The Learning Phase:**
    * Highly structured and rigid.
    * Comprises mandatory live classes, homework, and evaluation tests.
2.  **The Job Search / Support Phase:**
    * Light, flexible structure.
    * Focuses on continued self-driven practice, application strategies, and live mock interviews.

## 5. Weekly Class & Lab Schedule (IST)

* **Sundays (9:00 AM – 1:00 PM IST):**
    * *Live Project Building Labs* managed by Tech Leads (TLs) and Hiring Managers (HMs).
    * Interactive live discussions and deep dives into hands-on project engineering.
* **Wednesdays (8:00 PM – 9:00 PM IST):**
    * *Live AMA / Doubt-Clearing Sessions*.
    * Domain and industry-specific guidance from FAANG+ TLs, HMs, and sector experts.
* **Saturdays (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM IST):**
    * *Assignments & Case Study Discussions*.
    * Mentorship and code/design reviews with industry experts.
* **Asynchronous Content (Flexible Timeline):**
    * Self-paced viewing of foundational videos and completing assigned homework during personal focus hours.

## 6. Non-Academic & Administrative Support Systems

### 👥 Student Success Advocates (SSAs)
* **Scope:** Assist with all operational, behavioral, and logistical elements outside of tech/career pipelines (e.g., Time Management, Study Strategies, Motivation, Financing/Loan Partners, and Payment Plans).
* **Availability:** 7 days a week, **9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST**.
* **Access:** Reached via the **"Get Support"** tab on the UpLevel platform.

### 💼 Career Transformation Managers (CTMs)
* **Assignment Timeline:** Formally assigned **AFTER** your live classes conclude and you transition into the Job Search Phase.
* **Core Responsibilities:** Strategic resume development, corporate target shortlisting, job sourcing, matched lead identification, and application support.
* **Early Job Hunting:** Initiating job searches prematurely is allowed but discouraged, as it detracts focus from core technical prep.
* **Workflow Integration:**
    * Keep your CTM updated instantly upon receiving or completing external interviews.
    * Schedule mock interviews *prior* to facing live company rounds.
    * Participate in a **debrief call** with your CTM immediately after every real interview for personalized pipeline adjustments.

---

## 7. Important Operational Rules

* **Program Pauses:** Under no circumstances can the program or access timelines be paused.
* **Support Window:** Backed by a strict **6-Month Support Period** post-class completion.
* **Support Phase Utilization:** Use this window to review missed recorded sessions, participate in technical/career coaching, and schedule mock interviews.
* **Alternative Content Requests:** IK can provision recorded lectures from alternative faculty or arrange specialized content if it already exists or was pre-recorded on another topic.
* **Mock Conversion Flexibility:** Standard mock interviews can be explicitly converted into personalized **Mentoring Sessions** if preferred.
* **Proactive Communication:** The onus is on the student to inform IK staff immediately if help or operational intervention is needed.
