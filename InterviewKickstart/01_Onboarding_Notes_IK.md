# Interview Kickstart (IK) Program 2026
## Onboarding Call Summary & Portal Navigation Guide



## 1. Platform & Portal Navigation (Tabs Overview)

### 📊 Momentum Tab
* **Purpose:** Dedicated to tracking your day-to-day activities, assignments, and problem-solving progress.

### 💼 Interviews Tab
* **Activation:** Will be dynamically added to your dashboard after the **Data Engineering (DE) Program** officially starts.
* **Features:** * Allows regional selection (**Region can be selected** to align with localized hiring markets).
  * Includes **20 Mock Interviews**.
  * Extended platform/interview support for **+6 Months after program completion**.

### 📚 Library Tab
* **Core Functions:** * Access to bookmarks and notes buttons utilized during live sessions or problem-solving activities.
  * **Additional Resources:** Supplementary materials are appended here *based on request*.

### 🤝 Expert Connect Tab
* **Activation:** Appears after fulfilling specific course milestones.
* **Sub-Services:**
  * **Tech Connect:** Dedicated to technical preparation such as **DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms)** and **System Design**.
  * **Career Coaching:** Focused on interview-related strategy, resume polishing, and soft skills.
  * **Duration:** **Both unlimited till end of 6 months after program completion.** 
---

## 2. Program Timeline & Milestone Triggers

* **Placement Coordinator Engagement:** Triggered automatically after completing **80% of the course** and taking **3–4 Mock Interviews**.
* **Career Manager Support:** A Career Manager will also connect with you around this milestone to align your job search pipeline.
* **Support SLA:** The Operations Team is your primary point of contact (POC). Queries raised via **"Get Support"** feature a **2–3 hours turnaround time (TAT)**.
* **Referral Benefit:** **Referral ₹18k Amazon Voucher**

---

## 3. Curriculum Components & Onboarding Steps

### 🛠️ Initial Actions Required
1. Go through the foundational **Onboarding Video**.
2. Complete and submit the **Onboarding Form**.
3. **Discord Integration:**
   * Join the official Discord server using the provided invite link.
   * Fill out the **mandatory form** to be assigned to your specific **cohort**.
   * Walk through the integrated Discord tutorial to understand communication channels.

### 🤖 Course Content & Self-Paced Materials
* **Foundational Material 2026:** *Applied Agentic AI for SWEs* is provided as foundational content.
* **Agentic AI Playbook:** Fully self-paced. Can be completed simultaneously alongside your main track.
* **Session Recording SLA:** Live session recordings are uploaded to the portal within **24–48 hours** post-class.
* **Feedback Loop:** A **Class Feedback form** must be completed after *every single session*.
