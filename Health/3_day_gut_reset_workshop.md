# 🌿 3-Day De-Bloat Reset

> *Repeat for 7 days for a deeper gut cleanse*

-----

## ✨ What 3 Days Can Do

In just 3 days, you’ll usually notice:

- 💧 Less water retention
- 🫶 Flatter stomach
- 🪶 Reduced heaviness
- ✅ Better bowel movement

> **Best for:** Bloating caused by overeating, excess salt/sugar/alcohol, late nights, or festive food.

-----

## 🌅 Every Morning

*Pick **ONE** option — stick to it for all 3 days*

|Option                                |Recipe                                                    |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
|Warm Jeera Water                      |½ tsp cumin soaked overnight → boil → strain → sip warm   |
|Warm Ginger Water                     |3–4 ginger slices in 1 cup water → boil 3–4 min → sip warm|
|Apple Cider Vinegar Water *(optional)*|1 tsp ACV in 1 cup warm water → sip slowly                |


> ❌ **No lemon on an empty stomach** if you’re bloated or acidic.

-----

## 💧 Drink Through the Day

### Cumin–Fennel–Ajwain Water

Boil ½ tsp cumin + ½ tsp fennel + ¼ tsp ajwain in 1 litre water. Sip warm throughout the day.

### Other Drinks

- 🥥 **Coconut water** — once daily, mid-morning (200–250 ml)
- 🍵 **Peppermint / chamomile tea** — after meals if feeling heavy
- 💧 **Plain water** — sip slowly, don’t chug

> ❌ **Avoid:** Cold drinks, soda, packaged juices, alcohol

-----

## 🍽️ Meals

### 🌄 Breakfast *(Keep it light)*

|Option              |Details                                                                                |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Steamed Apple / Pear|Chop, steam or microwave till soft, sprinkle cinnamon                                  |
|De-Bloat Fruit Bowl |Papaya, pineapple, or kiwi — no salt, no chaat masala                                  |
|Thin Vegetable Poha |Rinsed thin poha + ghee + mustard + curry leaves + hing + carrots/beans/lauki + peanuts|
|Moong Dal Chilla    |Blend soaked yellow moong dal with ginger & jeera → pan-cook with minimal oil          |


> ❌ No bread, butter, or heavy milk. No raw onion in poha.

-----

### 🌞 Mid-Morning *(Pick one)*

- 🥥 Coconut water
- 🍎 One fruit
- 🌰 5–6 soaked almonds

-----

-----

## 🥗 Foods That Help De-Bloat

**Eat more of:** Lauki, tori, papaya, pineapple, ginger, homemade curd, rice (preferred over roti)

-----

## 🚫 Foods to Avoid

Maida · Cheese · Deep-fried food · Excess sugar · Raw onion at night · Excess salt

-----

## 💪 Protein Note *(Important)*

> ❌ **Skip protein powders for 3 days** — they often cause gas and bloating.

**Choose instead:** Moong dal · Homemade curd · Eggs (if you eat them) · Small amounts of fresh paneer/tofu

**Avoid for now:** Rajma · Chole · Chana · Soya chunks · Excess nuts · Cheese

-----

## 🌛 Night Routine

- 🌡️ Sip warm water
- 🚶 5–10 minute walk after dinner
- 🛋️ Don’t lie down immediately after eating

-----
